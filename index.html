Beyond the ordinary

A new perspective on space tourism

From Desert to Space explores how technology and innovation are opening new possibilities for humanity beyond Earth. The conference aims to bring together people and organizations interested in the future of space tourism, creating opportunities to exchange knowledge, discover ideas and build meaningful connections.
